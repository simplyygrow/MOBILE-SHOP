Growth Roadmap

4 phases mein aapki shop ko Udaipur ki #1 mobile repair brand banayenge — systematically aur measurably.

Month 1 — Foundation

Brand Setup & Digital Go-Live

Brand kit complete karo (logo, colors, templates), GMB profile fully optimize karo, Instagram + Facebook professional setup, pehle 8–10 posts publish, content calendar ready, WhatsApp Business activate karo. Baseline analytics setup karein.

Month 2 — Awareness Push

Content, Influencer & Ads Launch

2 influencer collaboration videos post karo, 3 professional content videos release karo, Meta ads ₹5,000–₹10,000 budget mein start karo (Udaipur local targeting), weekly posting schedule maintain karo, Google reviews campaign shuru karo.

Month 3 — Optimization

Scale What's Working

Best performing content boost karo, ad targeting refine karo, Google reviews 20+ laane ka campaign, referral system activate karo, local Udaipur WhatsApp groups mein presence banao, competitor gap analysis karo.

Month 4–6 — Dominance

Udaipur #1 Mobile Repair Brand

Seasonal campaigns run karo (exam season phone rush, monsoon water damage, Diwali gifting), repeat customer loyalty offers, word-of-mouth flywheel activate karo, brand collab + partnership opportunities explore karo Udaipur mein.
